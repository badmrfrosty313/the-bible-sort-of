# The Rock — Part 3: Payoff and Callbacks

At that exact moment, God walks into frame. He stops, looks at Adam, looks at the Rock, then looks back at Adam.

**GOD:** Adam.  
Adam looks up happily.  
**ADAM:** God.

God gestures.

**GOD:** What are you doing?

Adam gestures toward the Rock as though the answer is painfully obvious.

**ADAM:** Talking.

God looks at the Rock.

**GOD:** To that?

Adam nods.

God studies him for a moment, then crouches beside them. He looks at the Rock, then back at Adam.

**GOD:** That's a rock, Adam.

Silence.

Adam looks at the Rock, then God, then the Rock again. Something clicks. His face lights up.

**ADAM:** Rock.

God pauses.

**ADAM:** Rock.

God nods.

**GOD:** Yes.

Adam looks delighted.

**ADAM:** I know another word.

God looks at him. For once, no exhaustion. A small smile.

**GOD:** You do.

Adam pats the Rock.

**ADAM:** Rock.

God stands.

**GOD:** Come on.

Adam gets up and follows. After a few steps, Adam looks back.

**ADAM:** Bye, Rock.

God stops walking, does not turn around, waits a beat, then keeps going.

## After the Rock Conversation

Every later interaction becomes mercifully short.

First repeat:

**ADAM:** Hello, Rock.  
**GOD, IMPOSSIBLY DISTANT:** NO.

Adam smiles.

Another repeat:

**ADAM:** Rock.  
Silence.  
**ADAM:** Good.

After Adam receives naming authority:

**ADAM:** I can name things now.

A naming menu begins to appear:
- Rock
- Friend
- Adam
- Jeff

Before the player can select anything:

**GOD, OFFSCREEN:** DON'T.
