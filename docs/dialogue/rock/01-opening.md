# The Rock — Part 1: Opening and Early Branches

Adam approaches a conspicuously obvious rock. He stops and looks at it. The Rock remains extremely committed to being a rock.

**ADAM:** Hello.  
Silence. Adam waits.  
**ADAM:** Adam.  
Silence. Adam smiles.  
**ADAM:** You are quiet.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** God is not quiet.

Adam looks around to make sure God is not immediately nearby.

**ADAM:** God knows many words.  
He thinks.  
**ADAM:** I know Adam.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** And God.  
Another pause.  
**ADAM:** And tree.

Adam points toward a tree.

**ADAM:** Tree.

He looks back at the Rock.

**ADAM:** You are not tree.

A small realization.

**ADAM:** Good.

## Choice
- KEEP TALKING
- LEAVE

If the player leaves, Adam walks away. The conversation resumes from this point when the player returns.

If the player keeps talking, Adam sits beside the Rock.

**ADAM:** There is also apple.  
He thinks about this.  
**ADAM:** Apple is food.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** I learned that after I ate it.

Adam looks at the Rock seriously.

**ADAM:** You should not eat the stick.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** It is not food.  
Longer pause.  
**ADAM:** I learned that too.

## Choice
- KEEP TALKING
- PLEASE STOP

If **PLEASE STOP**:

**ADAM:** Stop.

He nods proudly.

**ADAM:** That is another word.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** God says it to me.

And he continues anyway.

Adam holds up both hands.

**ADAM:** I have hands.

He looks at one, then the other.

**ADAM:** Two.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** I think.

He looks at the Rock.

**ADAM:** You have no hands.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** That seems difficult.

Adam pats the Rock sympathetically.

**ADAM:** I can help.

He grabs it with both hands and strains. The Rock moves approximately two inches.

Adam sits back, deeply satisfied.

**ADAM:** There.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** Better.

## Choice
- ASK WHAT IT IS
- TELL IT ABOUT GOD
- TELL IT ABOUT ADAM
- LEAVE

### Ask What It Is

**ADAM:** What are you?  
Silence. Adam waits.  
**ADAM:** You do not know.

He nods sympathetically.

**ADAM:** I did not know either.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** Then God said Adam.

He points to himself.

**ADAM:** Adam.

He points at the Rock.

Long pause.

**ADAM:** Hmm.

### Tell It About God

**ADAM:** God made everything.

Adam gestures broadly at Eden.

**ADAM:** Tree.

He points elsewhere.

**ADAM:** Apple.

He points at himself.

**ADAM:** Adam.

He looks at the Rock.

Long pause.

**ADAM:** Probably you.

Adam leans closer.

**ADAM:** God knows things before I ask.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** Sometimes I ask anyway.  
Another beat.  
**ADAM:** He makes a face.

Adam attempts God's exhausted expression. It is terrible.

### Tell It About Adam

Adam points at himself.

**ADAM:** Adam is me.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** I learned that first.

He thinks.

**ADAM:** For a long time it was all I knew.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** It was enough.

Adam looks slightly proud of this.

After any branch, Adam settles beside the Rock.

**ADAM:** I saw a bird.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** It went up.

Adam points toward the sky.

**ADAM:** I cannot do that.

He looks at the Rock.

**ADAM:** You cannot either.

Adam smiles.

**ADAM:** We have that.
