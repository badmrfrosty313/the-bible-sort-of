# The Rock — Part 2: Existential Middle

## Choice
- CONTINUE
- FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, WALK AWAY

If the player chooses **FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, WALK AWAY**:

Adam looks around.

**ADAM:** God?

He waits. No response.

Adam turns back to the Rock.

**ADAM:** He is busy.

And he continues.

Adam looks toward the sky.

**ADAM:** Do you think the sky ends?  
Silence.  
**ADAM:** I asked God.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** He said no.

Adam stares upward for a while.

**ADAM:** That seems excessive.

He looks around Eden.

**ADAM:** There are many trees.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** One tree would have been easier.

He considers this.

**ADAM:** But then everyone would be at the same tree.

A longer pause. Adam slowly nods.

**ADAM:** Maybe God is right.

Beat. Adam leans toward the Rock and lowers his voice.

**ADAM:** Do not tell Him I said that.

Silence. Adam smiles.

**ADAM:** I knew I could trust you.

## Choice
- KEEP GOING
- I HAVE MADE A TERRIBLE MISTAKE

Both choices continue.

Adam lies back in the grass beside the Rock.

**ADAM:** I think God likes me.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** He makes this face sometimes.

Adam makes God's exhausted expression again.

**ADAM:** I do not know what it means.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** Maybe happy.

**GOD, OFFSCREEN:** It isn't.

Adam immediately sits upright and looks around. Nothing.

He looks back at the Rock.

**ADAM:** He hears very well.

Adam lowers his voice.

**ADAM:** Very well.

Adam studies the Rock again.

**ADAM:** Do you think God made you before me?  
Silence.  
**ADAM:** Probably.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** You seem older.

Adam pats it.

**ADAM:** Not bad old.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** Just...

Adam struggles for the word.

**ADAM:** Rock old.

He seems pleased with this phrase.

**ADAM:** I am Adam old.

Adam thinks.

**ADAM:** Adam old is not very old.

Another silence.

**ADAM:** Everything happened before me.

Adam looks around Eden.

**ADAM:** Tree was here.

He looks at the sky.

**ADAM:** Sky was here.

At the Rock.

**ADAM:** You were here.

Then at himself.

**ADAM:** Then Adam.

He sits with that.

**ADAM:** That is strange.  
Silence.  
**ADAM:** But now I am here.

Adam smiles.

**ADAM:** So that is good.

## Choice
- TELL THE ROCK A SECRET
- ASK IF YOU ARE FRIENDS
- LEAVE BEFORE THIS GETS WEIRDER

### Tell the Rock a Secret

Adam checks around him.

**ADAM:** I ate the stick twice.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** God only saw once.

Silence. Adam nods.

**ADAM:** Good.

### Ask If You Are Friends

Adam looks at the Rock.

**ADAM:** Are we friends?

Silence. Adam waits a surprisingly long time.

**ADAM:** I think yes.  
Pause.  
**ADAM:** I have never had a friend.

He considers that.

**ADAM:** I have also never not had a friend.

Adam frowns.

**ADAM:** This is difficult.

He pats the Rock.

**ADAM:** You understand.

### Leave Before This Gets Weirder

Adam starts to stand, stops, looks at the Rock, and sits back down.

**ADAM:** One more thing.

The branches converge. Adam is now completely comfortable beside the Rock.

**ADAM:** God says I need to learn.  
Beat.  
**ADAM:** I think I am learning.

He starts counting on his fingers.

**ADAM:** Adam. God. Tree. Apple. Bird. Hand. Sky.

He runs out of fingers, looks down at them, then starts over using the same fingers.

**ADAM:** Food. Stop. Up. Down.

He points at the Rock and pauses. He does not know the word.

**ADAM:** And you.
